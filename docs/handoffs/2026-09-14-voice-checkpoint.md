## Product and architecture accomplished

Maslow Voice is a conversational front end with an independent task path. The voice provider answers conversation and supplies a bounded work brief; a separate local Hermes coordinator owns execution. Ending the conversation releases audio without cancelling accepted work. This separation is implemented; universal A2A interoperability and direct routing to a user-selected coding agent without Hermes remain separate, unimplemented simplifications.
